Growing a business and running its operations are two different jobs.

Most founders don't burn out from the work that grows the business. They burn out from everything piling up around it.

  • Too much administrative work landing on your plate instead of someone else's
  • Inconsistent follow-ups with leads or clients because no one owns that process
  • Scheduling and coordination quietly eating hours every week
  • Client or project work getting delayed because the operational side can't keep up
  • No clear sense of which role to hire first, or whether hiring is even the right move yet
  • A previous assistant hire that didn't actually resolve the underlying problem

We help you find the work worth handing off — not just assign you an assistant.

Instead of a generic assistant, you get a specific recommendation: which tasks to delegate, and which kind of remote specialist actually fits.

Sometimes the clearer answer is a process fix, not a new hire. We'll tell you that too.

Frequently asked questions

Is the roadmap free?

Yes. There's no cost for the roadmap, and no obligation to work with us afterward.

What will I receive?

A short, written Personalized Delegation Roadmap: your primary bottleneck, 3–5 specific tasks worth delegating, the right support category for each, a priority level, a practical first step, and the questions worth resolving before you hire anyone.

What happens after I submit the form?

We'll follow up by email to confirm a few details and prepare your roadmap. If something's missing from what you shared, we'll ask directly rather than guessing.

Am I required to hire Flow Remote Ops?

No. The roadmap is yours either way. If hiring isn't the right move yet, we'll say so — the recommendation might just be a process fix.

What support categories are available?

Executive & administrative support, finance & bookkeeping, creative & content, systems & automation, lead generation & sales support, and customer support & other business operations functions — matched to the bottleneck identified in your roadmap.

How is this different from hiring a generic virtual assistant?

We start by identifying the actual bottleneck and the right fit for it, instead of handing you an assistant and hoping it works out.
